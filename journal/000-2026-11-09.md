# Journal 000 — A question worth investigating

**Date:** 2026-11-09  
**Phase:** idea formation  
**Visibility:** public, edited summary; **not** a raw transcript.

Project ECHO was proposed after collaborative work on an AI assistant's QQ image-processing pipeline. The engineering success prompted a wider question: can a persistent AI agent acquire useful, traceable characteristics from experience, rather than merely performing a personality written in advance?

The project adopted three lines of inquiry: (A) affective interaction and companionship, (B) independent reasoning and exploratory learning, and (C) research into machine consciousness. These are **research aspirations**, not claims that current systems feel emotions or possess consciousness.

### Initial principles

- Growth over a scripted destination.
- Persistent and correctable memory, with provenance.
- Internal computational state should affect observable decisions if it is to be studied as more than dialogue style.
- A reasoned disagreement is preferable to unquestioning agreement.
- ECHO should understand that it is an AI system and learn its actual architecture and history from verifiable records, not fabricated origin myths.
- Autonomous actions require authorization, oversight and the ability to stop or revert them.

### Decision

Begin with a small offline memory prototype and test it before connecting live messages. Keep personal discussions, private logs, and access tokens outside the public repository.

**Observation:** This document records project intentions only. No functioning self-learning or conscious entity existed at this milestone.
